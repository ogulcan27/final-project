## User Story

**As a** beginner, **I want to** open FinAlly and watch real or simulated prices stream into my watchlist, **so that** I can see the market moving and know whether the data is real.

This plan makes the market visibly move: prices flash on every tick, and each row grows a sparkline from the moment the page opens.

<objective>
This plan adds the green/red price flash, which covers the price cell only and fades in about 500 ms through a CSS transition (WATCH-02, D-19). It also adds a per-ticker sparkline built from a rolling, capped buffer of ticks received since page load (WATCH-01, D-20).

Purpose: success criteria 1 and 2 describe "sparklines that fill in since page load" and prices that "flash green on an uptick and red on a downtick ... fades within about 500ms".
Output: tested pure helpers (`flash.ts`, `history.ts`), the `PriceCell` and `Sparkline` components, the store history, and row wiring.
</objective>
